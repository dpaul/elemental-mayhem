⚡ 1. Turn Flow & Action Points (AP)

Every turn, your champion receives Action Points (AP). Moving costs 1 AP per tile. Casting spells consumes AP based on the ability's cost (1–3 AP). Unused AP is restored at the start of your next turn.

🔥 2. Elemental Matrix & Reactions

Hitting enemies with opposing elements triggers massive damage bonuses and status effects. For instance, striking a Frozen enemy with Fire triggers Thermal Shock, while striking an Undead foe with Light triggers Holy Smite!

🧟 3. Necromancy & Zombie Reanimation

When playing as the Necromancer, any enemy slain by necrotic abilities rises immediately as an allied Zombie with 4x HP, 3x Speed, and a 4-turn lifespan! When the zombie expires, it turns into bone piles and restores +3 AP to the Necromancer.

🌱 4. Life & Cascading Unzombify Explosions

The Biomancer (Life) wields the Unzombify Explosion: an AoE spell that cleanses zombies in a 3x3 area. Each unzombified zombie explodes in a burst of radiant Life, triggering chain-reaction detonations on nearby zombies and summoning an agile Being of Life (3x Speed) capable of transmuting zombies!
